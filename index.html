Stop Planning in the Dark

Traditional planning treats forecasting and optimization as disconnected steps, leaving analysts trapped in siloed files without version tracking or scenario lineage.

The Fragmented Reality LEGACY

Teams need reliable tools to forecast future conditions, compare options, and plan resources. But data, forecasts, and scenarios often sit in separate spreadsheets with no clear record of changes.

  • ✕ Models lack version history, making errors and changed assumptions harder to track.
  • ✕ Manual scenario comparisons make it difficult to weigh options side by side.
  • ✕ Missing decision records make it hard to explain which data and assumptions supported a plan.
  • ✕ Fixed planning limits make it difficult to adjust resource plans when market conditions change.

The Quantiva Approach UNIFIED

Connect raw data to forecasting models, resource optimization, and a traceable history of decisions through one complete workflow.

  • ✓ Automatically collect operational data over time and check that its fields follow a consistent structure.
  • ✓ Use ready-made statistical and GPU-powered models to produce forecasts with estimated ranges and measure accuracy using MAPE.
  • ✓ Balance multiple goals and calculate trade-offs within defined budget, capacity, and resource limits.
  • ✓ Compare scenarios side by side and keep a clear historical record of the assumptions, results, and decisions.

Workflow Architecture

From Raw Data to Better Decisions in Five Steps

A repeatable, governed operational protocol that eliminates guesswork and manual spreadsheet reconciliation.

01

Connect Datasets

Ingest transactional and sensor streams via secure SQL, CSV, or direct warehouse connectors with schema verification.

02

Configure Variables

Identify target metrics, temporal seasonality, and exogenous indicators like tariffs, prices, or ambient temperature.

03

Generate Forecasts

Execute GPU-accelerated forecasting models with automated hyperparameter sweeps and confidence bands.

04

Define Objectives & Constraints

Set optimization targets (e.g., maximize EBITDA or minimize load curtailment) bounded by budget and supply limits.

05

Compare Scenarios

Evaluate trade-offs side-by-side, store the audit decision record, and export reports for executive review.

Our Mission & Origins

Why Quantiva Exists

Founded by quantitative practitioners who experienced firsthand the operational risks of decision-making without unified analytical records.

Quantiva was founded on 14 August 2024 by Yathushan Krishnamoorthy to solve a pervasive vulnerability in institutional decision-making: finance, energy, and supply chain teams lacked a structured way to manage forecasting models and optimization scenarios with trustworthy history.

Our mission is to centralize forecasting and optimization workflows into a single rigorous workspace, making high-dimensional future projections effortless to model, critique, and review.

Everything you need to know about integrating Quantiva into your quantitative planning pipeline.

Quantiva is an AI-powered Quantitative Forecasting & Optimization platform engineered specifically for financial planning, energy utility management, and supply chain operations teams. It consolidates dataset management, forecasting project configuration, objective-constrained optimization scenarios, and permanent analytical audit records into a single rigorous workspace.
You can model univariate and multivariate time-series projections across financial metrics (revenue, cash burn, OPEX), energy indicators (grid load, solar/wind generation, spot tariffs), and supply chain flows (SKU demand, container transit times, warehouse inventory). Quantiva supports classical statistical engines, machine learning regressors, and GPU-accelerated neural architectures.
After generating a forecast, you configure mathematical objectives (such as maximizing portfolio return or minimizing grid transmission costs) subject to hard and soft constraints (such as maximum capital expenditure, statutory reserves, minimum inventory thresholds, or capacity ceilings).
